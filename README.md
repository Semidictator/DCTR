### 3.1.1 Forward Burn Policy

Starting with this revision, DCTR commits to a fixed, formulaic burn rate applied to every monthly unlock released from the Core Locked Treasury (Section 3.2): **9% of each monthly release is permanently burned** via the Solana Incinerator at the time of unlock, with the remaining 91% released as scheduled.

| Monthly unlock | Burned (9%) | Released (91%) |
|---|---|---|
| 20,000,000 DCTR | 1,800,000 DCTR | 18,200,000 DCTR |

*This policy is deflationary by design on top of the existing lock schedule: every unlock event permanently reduces total supply, rather than releasing the full amount into circulation. Actual burn transactions tied to each unlock are verifiable on-chain via the token contract (Section 4).*

### 3.1.2 Additional Burn Channels (Utility-Linked)

Beyond the scheduled monthly burn above, DCTR incorporates two further burn mechanisms that tie deflation directly to real platform engagement rather than the calendar alone:

- **Burn-to-Mint NFT Trophies:** minting or redeeming any NFT Trophy (Section 3.3) requires burning a corresponding amount of DCTR, set per trophy tier. This links scarcity directly to genuine participation and achievement inside the game, rather than to a fixed emission schedule alone.
- **Unclaimed Trophy Burn:** any portion of a seasonal reward pool left unclaimed at the close of a competitive season is automatically and permanently burned rather than rolled over or redistributed — rewards can never sit around as latent future sell-pressure.

*Together, the three mechanisms (scheduled unlock burn, burn-to-mint, and unclaimed-trophy burn) make DCTR's deflation multi-layered: part fixed and predictable, part driven entirely by community activity.*

### 3.1.3 Founder's Exceptional Decree (Discretionary Burn Event)

In keeping with the Founder's absolute authority (Section 2B), @drjnab retains the power to declare an **Exceptional Decree**: a one-off, publicly announced burn of some or all of a given month's unlock, above and beyond the standard 9% policy. This is a deliberate narrative and strategic tool, not a routine mechanism, and is bound by the following guardrails:

- **Occasion-triggered, not scheduled:** a Decree is tied to a specific milestone or event (e.g. a season launch, a major community achievement, a market event) — never a standing monthly default.
- **Publicly announced in advance:** the Founder announces the Decree and its exact burn amount before execution, keeping it verifiable on-chain like every other burn channel.
- **Operational budget is protected by default:** because the Core Locked Treasury also funds cabinet incentives, liquidity, and season rewards, a full-unlock Decree is understood to be rare and exceptional — repeated or routine use would undermine the very treasury the game depends on to run.

*The Exceptional Decree exists to give DCTR's satirical "absolute power" premise a real, on-chain consequence — while the guardrails above keep it from quietly becoming the default policy in place of the sustainable 9% mechanism.*

### 3.3 Trophy & Presidential Reserve (150,000,000 DCTR — Phase 2 Lock)

**Purpose:** dedicated as presidential trophies and rewards for presidential election cycles.

**Unlock timing:** planning and system design for this reserve begins 3 months after launch, aligned with the Core Locked Treasury's cliff expiration (Section 3.2). The first actual distribution, however, is deliberately deferred to **month 6 post-launch** — separated from the Core Treasury's initial unlock event to avoid compounding sell-pressure at a single moment, and to ensure enough verified in-game performance data exists before any reward is issued. From month 6 onward, distribution follows the 10-year schedule (15,000,000 DCTR/year) during presidential terms, to maintain hyper-long-term sustainability.

### 3.3.1 Distribution Mechanism — Merit-Based, Discretionary Allocation

Rather than a fixed top-down percentage split, distribution from this reserve is structured around earned merit and sector-level accountability:

- **Citizens holding trophies/badges (achievers):** the primary recipient pool. Allocation is weighted by verified in-game achievement, not by citizenship alone.
- **Non-achieving citizens ("Broke Citizens"):** receive a minimal, symbolic allocation — enough to remain part of the ecosystem, but deliberately smaller than the achiever pool.
- **Sector Presidents & Cabinet Ministers:** each Sector President holds full discretion over their sector's ministerial allocation. Presidents set individual performance targets ("Call-Out Targets") for each of their Ministers and reward them proportionally to their percentage of target achievement — functioning as a call-out-style performance bonus rather than a fixed salary.

*This structure intentionally mirrors the project's satirical premise: reward is not evenly distributed by default — it is earned, negotiated, and occasionally political, exactly like the systems DCTR satirizes.*
